Get help

Email cristofer.slotoroff@gmail.com or message Cris on LinkedIn. Bug reports with a screenshot and the time you saw the problem are the most helpful.

Common questions

  • No trolleys showing. PCC cars usually run from early morning into early evening, and not every day. Buses cover the route the rest of the time. Open "See Trolley Analytics" to see typical hours.
  • Times seem wrong. Positions come from SEPTA's public feed, which updates about once a minute and can lag. Arrival estimates are estimates.
  • Data unavailable. SEPTA's feed sometimes goes quiet. Pull to refresh or try again in a few minutes.
